The Kambridge location is designed for Stock Kerbin deployment only. 

The Runway for the Kambridge site is already included in the GAPFAS Navagation and autopilot patches. 
If you want to fly GAPFAS missions to and from the Kambridge Airport you will need to add the following site information to your GAPFASContractGroupPatch connfig


	siteFullName:	Kambridge
	siteName:		KBR 	
	siteLAT:		35.1027908325195
	siteLON:		9.19531726837158 
	terminalLAT:	35.0493393090519 
	terminalLON: 	9.24526667952929
	siteMultiFlight: (Choose any base index number in your patch file that you want to fly to from Kambridge. Do not choose 0)
